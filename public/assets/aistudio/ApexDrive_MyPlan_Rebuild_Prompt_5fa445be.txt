0. THE GOLDEN RULE: CODE CALCULATES, AI ADVISES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
All times, distances, durations, radius bands, availability, slot suggestions, reordering and recalculation are done by the deterministic SCHEDULING ENGINE (plain TypeScript, unit-tested).

The AI (Gemini) NEVER calculates times, distances or whether something fits. The AI only:
- suggests WHO to contact and why (one plain sentence)
- suggests the method and purpose
- drafts emails, texts and talking points
- writes plain-English explanations

The engine validates everything, and its numbers always win. With AI off, My Plan works fully.

2. DISTANCE BANDS (from the rep's HOME, always)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  🟢 HOME REGION   0–60 miles
  🟡 EDGE          60–69 miles  (10–15% tolerance; allowed, with a gentle "Edge of your region" flag)
  🔴 OUT OF TOWN   70+ miles    (a travel prospect; handled as a TRIP, section 3)

- Straight-line distance decides the band. Drive time is used for scheduling.
- Thresholds are editable in Settings.
- The same engine function is used everywhere: My Plan, pool, claiming, suggestions, AI, map and reports.
- Lists and suggestions show the home region first.

- Trips show as a banner across the day(s) in Day / Week / Month.

6. MULTIPLE SELECTION CREATES SEPARATE SLOTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Multiple selection is ONLY a shortcut for adding several items at once. Every selected dealership becomes its OWN SEPARATE SLOT, with its own:
- dealership, contact, purpose
- time (or send time)
- duration
- status
- message (pre-filled from the shared template, editable per slot)
- outcome / log
- points

Examples:
- 8 emails selected → 8 separate email slots. Send times are staggered sensibly (e.g. a few minutes apart), within the recommended send window and Gmail limits.
- A call list of 6 → 6 separate call slots, placed back to back (with a short gap) in the live window.
- 5 texts → 5 separate text slots, each opening its own pre-filled message on the phone when it's time.

Each slot can be moved, reordered, rescheduled, edited, skipped or removed individually.

Optional visual grouping: consecutive slots created together can show a small header ("8 emails") that can be collapsed or expanded. Grouping is display-only; the slots stay separate underneath.

Email is sent individually through Gmail (never one email with everyone on it). Text becomes a queue: each message opens in the phone's messaging app, pre-filled. No fake "Send all" button. Leave a marked hook for a future texting service.

7. REARRANGING SLOTS (arrows AND drag and drop, everywhere)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Every slot card has ▲ / ▼ arrow buttons (always visible, large, labelled "Move up" / "Move down" for screen readers) AND a drag handle. The arrows are the main method for less confident users; drag and drop is for everyone else. Both work on mobile (touch) and desktop (mouse and keyboard).
- When the order changes, the ENGINE immediately recalculates:
  - times
  - travel between items
  - the leave-by time
  - finish and home times
  - how full the day is
  Then it shows what changed in plain words (e.g. "Smith Motors is now 11:15. Your day ends 10 min later. · Undo").
- LOCKED slots (🔒, e.g. a confirmed appointment or a Manager Assist meeting) keep their time. Other slots flow around them. Moving a slot across a locked one is allowed, and times adjust around the lock.
- If the new order breaks a rule (e.g. a visit is pushed past 4 PM, or a drive can't be made in time), the move still happens. The engine shows a gentle warning with one-tap fixes: "Put it back", "Move to tomorrow", "Turn into a phone call".
- Moving between days:
  - In the Day view, the card's actions include "Move to another day" (big day buttons: Tomorrow / pick a day).
  - In the Week view, cards can be dragged between days, and each card also has a "Move to…" button.
  - Moved items get a fresh slot suggestion on their new day.
- Unscheduled items live in a "Not scheduled yet" tray at the top of the Week view and the bottom of the Day view, and can be dragged or arrow-moved into a day.
- Undo is available for every move.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
8. TIME RULES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
All times are in the DEALERSHIP'S local time zone. The rep's own time is also shown when it differs.

- LIVE CONTACT WINDOW, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM: for ALL person-to-person contact (in-person visits, phone calls, Google Meet and any other live meeting). The engine never SUGGESTS live contact outside the window. If the rep chooses it anyway, it's allowed with a gentle note ("Dealerships are usually busy before 10 AM").
- NON-LIVE:
  - Writing emails and texts, reading briefs, notes and admin are suggested OUTSIDE the live window (before 10, after 4, at lunch, in gaps).
  - Sending happens at the recommended time: emails are sent automatically (Apps Script time-based trigger or a backend queue; the rep can see, edit or cancel them), and texts become a reminder that opens the pre-filled message.
  - Editable defaults in Settings, learned from reply rates over time:
    - Email: Tue–Thu, about 10:00–11:30 AM or 2:00–3:00 PM; avoid Monday mornings and Saturdays.
    - Text: Tue–Fri, 10:30 AM–3:30 PM.
    - Live contact: best Tue–Thu, then Mon / Fri. Saturday off by default. Working days are set in Settings.
- LEAVE-BY: first in-person appointment − drive time from home − 10 min parking / walk-in − 10 min safety margin. It's shown at the top of the day, with an optional reminder 15 min before. Between visits, show "Leave by 11:40 to reach Lone Star Ford". The estimated arrival home is shown at the end of the day.

9. DURATIONS AND TRAVEL (engine)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Defaults (editable in Settings and per slot; learned from actual logged times):
  - First prospect visit 45 min · follow-up visit 30 · client care visit 30 · Keyfetch top-up visit 20
  - Contract signing 45 · onboarding training 60
  - Phone call 15 · Google Meet 30
  - Email slot 5 min (prep) · text slot 2 min
- Travel: Google Maps drive times when a key is set. Otherwise: straight-line distance × 1.3, at about 30 mph in metro areas and 50 mph rural, rounded up to the next 5 min, plus a 10 min parking / walk-in buffer. Shown between cards.
- Other blocks the rep can add, so the day is never overbooked: Lunch (on by default, 30 min, flexible around noon), Admin, Team meeting, Personal, Office.

10. WORKING THROUGH THE DAY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- In the Day view for today, the next slot is highlighted as "NEXT", with ONE big button for the next step:
  - In person: "Start driving" (opens Maps) → "I've arrived" → "Done – how did it go?"
  - Phone call: "Call now" (tap to call) → "How did it go?"
  - Google Meet: "Join meeting" → "How did it go?"
  - Email: "Review & send" (or "Scheduled for 10:15 AM", with Edit / Cancel)
  - Text: "Send text" (opens the pre-filled message) → "Done"
- "How did it go?":
  - big outcome buttons (Prospects: Interested / Not now / Needs follow-up / Hot; Clients: All good / Top-up done / Upsell interest / Issue to fix)
  - a quantity box for a Keyfetch top-up
  - a voice or text note
  - "Plan the next step?", which reuses the Add flow pre-filled
  Points are shown with a short celebration.
- "I need more time" (+15 / +30) during a visit: the engine recalculates the rest of the day immediately and explains the effect. If something now breaks a rule, it offers: keep, move to tomorrow, turn into a call, or swap the order.
- Can't do it? "Later today" / "Tomorrow" / "Pick a day" / "Turn into a call" / "Skip (with reason)". Skipped items go to "Not scheduled yet"; they're never lost.
- On mobile, a full-screen "Today" mode shows the same thing with bigger controls.

11. WEEK, MONTH, PAST DAYS, MANAGERS, SYNC
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- WEEK: day cards in a simple vertical list on mobile, or columns on desktop. Each shows the leave-by time, number of slots by work type, how full it is, the main area, and any trip banner. Plus the "Not scheduled yet" tray. Drag or arrow-move between days.
- MONTH: the overview (section 4), with colored dots per day.
- PAST DAYS: read-only diary showing planned vs done, outcomes, notes, synced Gmail / Meet / Chat activity and points, with a one-line summary. Late notes can be added.
- MANAGERS / TEAM LEADS / OWNERS:
  - can open any rep's My Plan (read-only)
  - see the team's week at a glance
  - push Manager Priorities into a rep's plan ("From Jason Scott" badge; the engine finds the slot; the rep accepts)

- Two-way Google Calendar sync. Calendar events appear as locked blocks.
- AI (advice only): "Suggested for you" in the Add flow, method suggestions, message drafts, and "Help me fill the gaps" (the AI suggests WHO; the engine decides WHERE; the rep accepts each one).
- Simple mode is ON by default for new reps. Full mode (denser cards, keyboard shortcuts) is optional for veterans. A short first-time walkthrough (3–4 screens, replayable from Help).
