GOAL
The CRM is a tool our reps, managers and owners use all day to plan and optimize their day, week and month with AI, while EARNING POINTS for doing the right things. It must become far more engaging both for people WORKING in it during their day and for people VIEWING it for reporting.

1. NO MONETARY / FINANCIAL DATA (global rule)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The system must NOT capture or store any monetary or financial details, anywhere.

Remove from the UI, types, storage adapters, the Google Sheets schema / setup script, seed data, Workspace mock data, exports and AI prompts:
- deal values
- prices, unit prices and pricing models
- "pricing discussed"
- revenue
- restock dollar totals
- dealership-related payout amounts (incentive payouts are the exception; see section 4)
- incentive spend and ROI
- any $ figures

EXCEPTION: incentive dollar amounts ARE stored and shown, under the visibility rules in section 4. This is the only monetary data allowed in the system.

Make sure the AI never generates or displays money figures either.

Replace money-based metrics with counts and quantities:
- number of prospects, visits, deals won and clients signed
- products signed per client
- product penetration
- upsells won (count)
- Keyfetch units restocked (quantity)
- conversion rates
- cycle times

Pipeline cards and prioritization use stage, heat, relationship temperature, dealership size (rooftops, used inventory) and likelihood to close, never value.

Seed data: strip every $ value. Fix any text like "$3,800 VSC package".

3. FLOW CHANGE: "HANDOFF" BECOMES "MANAGER ASSIST" (rep keeps ownership)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
When a prospect is HOT, the rep does NOT hand the deal over. The rep asks for the manager's HELP: the manager joins calls or meetings to help close. The REP KEEPS OWNERSHIP all the way to closure, unless a manager explicitly transfers it.

- Rename "Handoff" to "Manager Assist" everywhere (UI, nav, types, Sheets, seed data, AI prompts):
  - Manager nav: "Assist Requests"
  - Rep nav: "My Assists"
  - List the final names in your plan.
- Pipeline: remove the "Handed Off" stage. Manager Assist is a FLAG / badge that can sit on HOT, Negotiation or Contract, so the deal stays in its real stage on the REP's board, planner and client pool.
- Assist Request (raised by the rep from the deal):
  - Assist type: join a call / join an in-person visit / join a Google Meet / terms approval / strategy session / contract signing support.
  - Proposed times, respecting the 10 AM–4 PM dealership-local window for visits.
  - An AI-drafted briefing (decision maker, objections, products of interest, history; no pricing or money), editable by the rep. No hardcoded placeholder text.
  - The manager defaults to the rep's actual manager (not a hardcoded default). The rep can pick another manager or owner.
- Manager response:
  - Accept / Propose another time / Decline with a note.
  - On accept, the meeting goes onto BOTH Planners and Google Calendar.
  - The outcome is logged as ONE joint activity with both participants.
- Ownership:
  - assignedRepId stays the rep. Remove the logic that makes the manager the current closer.
  - Only a manager or owner can TRANSFER OWNERSHIP, via an explicit action with a required reason. It is audited and the rep is notified.
  - Origin-rep attribution NEVER changes.
- At Won / Signed, record who closed and who signed: rep, manager, or "closed with assist from…". The client lands in the origin rep's client pool.
- Reporting:
  - assist requests per rep and manager
  - manager response time
  - assist-to-win conversion
  - deals closed solo vs with assist
  - manager assist load
  - ownership transfers (with reasons)
- Seed several assists in every state, including one ownership transfer.

4. INTRODUCTION INCENTIVES (with or without an assist)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The introduction incentive must NOT depend on a handoff or assist. Many deals are scouted and closed by the rep alone, and finance must still be able to follow the trail and approve the incentive.

- Every prospect has an INTRODUCTION TRAIL built automatically from the audit log, shown as a clear visual timeline:
  source (Pool / Assigned / Manual Search) → claimed by → visits (dates, good/bad window) → product interest recorded → assist requested / completed (ONLY if it happened) → won / closed by → signed by → products signed → onboarding.
- Incentive eligibility is created automatically by configurable trigger events (Settings → Incentive Rules), e.g. "introduction qualified", "deal signed", "onboarding complete". It applies whether the deal was solo or assisted, and records whether an assist was involved.
- Finance controller (Donna Clark) workflow:
  - A queue of eligible introductions, each showing the full trail, the origin rep, who closed and signed, the assist involvement (if any), and any conflicts (e.g. two reps touched it, or an ownership transfer).
  - She can Verify / Approve / Reject / Mark disputed (with comments), and mark as Paid outside the system (date and optional external reference only).
  - Incentive states: Eligible → Pending verification → Verified → Approved → Paid / Rejected / Disputed.
  - INCENTIVE AMOUNTS: each introduction incentive has a dollar amount. Finance sets and edits it, optionally suggested from the Incentive Rules in Settings (amounts vary per introduction). Paid records store the amount, date and an optional external reference.
- VISIBILITY OF AMOUNTS (strict exception to the CRM's transparency):
  - A sales rep sees ONLY their own incentive amounts.
  - Managers, owners / executives and the finance controller see ALL amounts.
  - Team leads and other reps never see anyone else's amounts. They still see the introduction's status and trail, but the amount is hidden (no placeholder hinting at it).
- Enforce this everywhere amounts could appear: screens, record timelines, feeds, notifications, leaderboards, search and command bar results, AI answers and summaries, exports and reports. AI must not reveal an amount to anyone not allowed to see it.
- Enforce it in the data layer too, not just by hiding it in the UI. The Apps Script API only returns amount fields to permitted users. The CRM_Finance spreadsheet is shared only with finance, managers and owners; reps get their own amounts through the API, never by direct sheet access.
- Every change to an amount is audited (who, when, old value, new value).
- Reps see their own introductions and the status of each. Finance can export the approved list with amounts for the period. Managers and owners can also export with amounts. Reps can export only their own statement.
- Remove the old logic that only created an incentive when a handoff was raised, and remove the hardcoded $300 / "$250 base + $50" rule.

5. LIVE GAMIFICATION ENGINE (points visible everywhere, earned from real actions)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Currently, points, levels, ranks and streaks are static seed values on one page, and nothing awards points. Make gamification a LIVE system woven through the whole app.

Borrow these patterns from our other internal prototype ("VibeCRM"), adapted to ApexDrive and field sales:
- A persistent GAME STRIP in the top bar on every page: level number and title, points with a progress bar to the next level, points TODAY vs today's target (progress ring), streak (flame), team rank (#), and the active multiplier or bonus.
- Every action button shows what it earns ("Log visit +40", "Request assist +25", "Log Keyfetch top-up +30"), using live values from the Game Rules (section 6).
- Animated "+40 pts • Logged visit at Smith Motors" toasts. Confetti and a celebration banner for wins, signings, upsell wins, level-ups and badges. Optional sound, off by default.
- Levels with fun but professional field-sales titles (e.g. Rookie → Road Warrior → Lot Legend → Closer → Territory Boss → Hall of Fame).
- Badges with progress bars, locked vs unlocked, and categories (Scouting, Visits, Relationships, Closing, Servicing, Upselling, Teamwork). Include product badges, e.g. "Ceramic Pro Pioneer: first 3 Ceramic Pro upsells".
- A leaderboard with a top-3 podium, weekly / monthly / quarterly / season views, filters by region and role, rank movement arrows, and "Kevin just passed you" nudges.
- A points ledger per user (action, record, rule version, timestamp). Each dealership shows the points earned on it.

Default earning actions (values are your call; all editable in section 6):
- claiming a prospect
- completing a dealership profile
- recording product interest
- a completed in-person visit WITH notes and an outcome (bonus inside 10 AM–4 PM, reduced outside it)
- logged calls, emails and Meet calls (quality-weighted; no points for trivial emails)
- on-time follow-ups; clearing overdue ones
- moving a deal forward (no points for stage ping-pong)
- requesting an assist with a complete brief (rep); accepting and attending one (manager)
- won, signed and onboarding complete (rep and manager split when assisted)
- each product signed at sign-up
- upsell won (per product, with product bonuses)
- Keyfetch top-up logged
- client health improved; a re-engaged at-risk client
- completing manager priorities (section 7)
- following the day plan (e.g. 80%+ of stops completed)
- streaks and daily quests

Fair play: points only for verified, quality actions; daily caps; undo reverses points; no points for duplicate or empty logs. Rookie league and personal bests for new reps. Managers and owners earn points too, and leaderboards can show "reps only" or "everyone".

Derive all points, levels, ranks and streaks from the Points_Ledger, generated from the seeded activity history using the default rules, so the numbers are consistent across the app.

6. GAME RULES CONSOLE (owners and managers change how points are earned)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Add a "Game Rules" area under Settings.
- Owners can edit everything.
- Managers can create product bonuses, quests and challenges for their team or region.
- Everyone else can view the rules (transparency).

The console contains:
- POINTS TABLE: every scorable action, with points, enabled/disabled, daily cap, and which roles it applies to.
- PRODUCT BONUSES: managers can pick ANY catalog product (existing or newly added) and set bonus points or a multiplier, e.g.:
  - "+150 bonus for every Ceramic Pro upsell won"
  - "2x on GAP sign-ups this month"
  - "+50 for recording interest in EV Solutions"
  Choose which actions it applies to (interest recorded / signed at sign-up / upsell won). Set the start and end dates, and target everyone, a region, a team or specific reps.
  Active product bonuses are highlighted on the game strip, in the product checkboxes ("🔥 2x"), in upsell suggestions and in the AI plan.
  When a NEW product is added to the catalog, prompt the manager: "Create a launch bonus?"
- MULTIPLIERS: good visit window, bad-time visits, new territory, region or team boost.
- LEVELS, BADGES and STREAK rules (thresholds, titles, criteria, grace days).
- TARGETS: daily and weekly point targets per role.
- QUESTS and CHALLENGES: create, schedule and target them; head-to-head and team-vs-team sprints.
- SEASONS: quarterly resets, with an all-time hall of fame.
- MANUAL ADJUSTMENTS: add or remove points with a required reason; audited.
- SIMULATE: preview how the leaderboard would have looked under the changed rules before saving.
- VERSIONING: rules are versioned with effective dates, and ledger entries store the rule version.
- Points are NOT money, and no money is linked to them (section 1).

Store all of this in CRM_Gamification sheets: Point_Rules, Product_Bonuses, Multipliers, Levels, Badges, Quests, Challenges, Seasons, Points_Ledger, Rule_Versions.

7. AI COACH + MANUAL CONTROL + MANAGER PRIORITIES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The AI suggests; humans stay in control. Business value comes FIRST: the AI must never push points over an important client, deal or communication.

AI coach:
- "Today's Game Plan":
  - Ordered good-window stops; calls and emails before 10 AM / after 4 PM; assists; Keyfetch top-ups due; overdue follow-ups; upsells (highlighting active product bonuses); manager priorities.
  - Each item shows WHY it matters and the points it's worth, plus the day's projected points vs target.
  - The same for week and month.
- Daily quests from real data (e.g. "Log notes for yesterday's 2 visits", "Reply to the email promised to Smith Motors on Monday", "Pitch Ceramic Pro to 2 clients: 2x bonus").
- Live nudges, e.g.:
  - "2 more good-window visits keeps your streak"
  - "60 pts from passing Todd Kelly"
  - "Lone Star Ford is 8 min away and due a Keyfetch top-up"
  - "Meet notes not logged"
- Missed-communication radar: commitments found in emails, Meet notes and chats that haven't been actioned; prospects and clients going quiet (ghosting risk); overdue follow-ups. One click to act.
- Relationship temperature (from VibeCRM's sentiment idea):
  - A 0–100 score and trend sparkline per dealership, built from synced emails, Meet notes and visit notes.
  - Highlighted intent / hesitation / urgency / friction phrases.
  - Ghosting risk.
  - Feeds lead scoring and the plan.
- End-of-day, weekly and monthly recaps: points, plan vs actual, wins, what slipped, and the next draft plan.

MANUAL CONTROL (important):
- Reps can build their day, week or month plan completely by hand, or start from the AI plan and edit it: add, remove, reorder or reschedule stops (drag and drop), add custom tasks and personal blocks (e.g. "office admin 9–10"), change visit durations, and set start and end locations.
- PIN / LOCK items, so the AI must keep them when regenerating ("Regenerate around my locked items").
- An AI on/off preference per user. With AI off, the Planner still works fully as a manual planner and calendar.
- Override any AI suggestion (dismiss with an optional reason); the AI learns from dismissals in future suggestions.
- Every AI-generated item is labelled as such. Manual items are never overwritten without confirmation.

MANAGER PRIORITIES (managers / owners suggest things of importance):
- A manager can push a "Priority" to one rep, several reps, a region or the whole team. For example:
  - "Visit Smith Motors this week: decision maker back from vacation"
  - "Focus on EV Solutions interest in Dallas"
  - "Call all Keyfetch clients due for top-up"
  It includes a reason, a priority level (Normal / High / Urgent), a due date or window, and optional linked dealership(s) and product(s), plus optional bonus points.
- Priorities appear in the rep's Planner and Today view with a distinct "From Jason Scott" badge. The AI plan MUST include them, scheduled sensibly.
- The rep can Accept, Schedule, Complete, or Decline / Snooze with a reason. The manager sees the status live.
- Team-wide FOCUS setting: managers can set a weekly focus (e.g. a product, area or type of client) that the AI weights in everyone's plans.
- Managers can also view, comment on, and suggest edits to any rep's plan (transparency). The rep sees the suggestions and accepts or declines them.
- Reporting: priorities sent, accepted, completed on time, declined (with reasons), per rep and manager.
